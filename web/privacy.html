Privacy Policy and Data Transparency

A plain-language explanation of what audio we capture, where files are stored, which third-party services process your data, retention schedules, and complete data deletion.

1. What is Recorded

When you initiate a recording via the DecisionAI Chrome Extension, the application captures two separate audio channels:

  • Call Audio (Participants): Audio played during the Google Meet call, routed so you can continue hearing the conversation through your speakers or headphones.
  • Microphone Audio (Your Voice): Your spoken voice captured through your microphone.

Both audio streams are converted into clear voice recordings for transcription. Video, screen sharing, camera feeds, chat messages, and browser cookies are never captured.

Participant Consent: Before recording can begin, the extension requires you to confirm that all participants in the meeting have been informed and provided consent. This consent confirmation is recorded on the meeting record.

2. Where Data is Stored

All data collected by DecisionAI is kept strictly within your designated secure storage:

  • Audio Recordings: Stored securely in your private application storage.
  • Meeting Records and Transcripts: Meeting details, speaker dialogue, summaries, and extracted decisions are stored in your secure account data.
  • Search Index: Processed meeting notes are indexed to enable conversational questions and answers in Ask AI.
  • Activity History: Important account actions (such as sign-ins, meeting creation, and deletions) are recorded for your security. Spoken dialogue and confidential meeting content are never stored in activity logs.

3. Third-Party Services Processing Your Data

DecisionAI uses targeted AI services strictly to transcribe speech and extract meeting insights:

  • Speech Recognition: Audio is submitted to your configured transcription engine (Google Gemini, Sarvam AI, Deepgram, or local offline processing) to convert spoken dialogue into text.
  • Meeting Insights: Meeting transcripts are analyzed to generate executive summaries, action items, key decisions, and answers to your questions in Ask AI.

We do not sell your data or use your confidential meetings to train public models. Audio and transcripts are transmitted over encrypted, secure connections only when processing is explicitly triggered.

4. Data Retention Schedules

You have full control over data retention. In Settings, you can select your preferred retention policy:

  • Keep Forever (Default): Data is preserved until you choose to delete it.
  • 30 Days: Recordings older than 30 days are automatically purged.
  • 90 Days: Recordings older than 90 days are automatically purged.
  • 365 Days: Recordings older than 1 year are automatically purged.

An automated daily cleanup job evaluates meeting start dates against your retention policy and purges expired meetings, audio recordings, and search data.

5. How to Delete Your Data

You have the right to erase your data at any time:

  • Delete Single Meeting: On the dashboard or meeting viewer page, click the "Delete Meeting" button. This immediately and permanently deletes the meeting details, transcript, extracted decisions, audio recordings, and search index entries.
  • Delete All My Data: In Settings, you can permanently erase your entire profile by typing the exact phrase DELETE ALL MY DATA. This completely removes all your meetings, transcripts, decisions, audio files, search data, activity history, and your user account, immediately signing you out.
